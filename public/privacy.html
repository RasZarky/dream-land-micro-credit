Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

Last updated: 16 August 2026

1. Who we are

This privacy policy explains how Dreamland Micro Lending Limited ("Dreamland", "we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects personal information when you visit our website or use our services. We are based in Sunyani, Bono Region, Ghana, and can be contacted at info@dreamlandmicro.com.

2. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • your name, phone number, and email address;
  • employment and income information;
  • details of your loan request, including amount, term, and purpose;
  • identification and supporting documents you provide when applying for a loan; and
  • any other information you choose to share with us.

We may also collect limited technical information automatically, such as your browser type and pages visited, to improve our website. See our Cookie Policy for more details.

3. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • process and assess your loan application;
  • communicate with you about your application, loan, or repayments;
  • verify your identity and financial information;
  • manage and collect repayments;
  • comply with legal and regulatory obligations; and
  • improve our products, services, and website.

4. Legal basis for processing

We process your personal information on the following bases: the performance of a contract with you (processing your loan application and administering your loan); compliance with legal obligations; and our legitimate interests in running our business, such as preventing fraud and improving our services.

5. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only where necessary:

  • with service providers who help us operate (for example, payment or IT providers), under contracts that require them to protect your information;
  • with credit reference bureaus or regulators, where required or permitted by law; and
  • with law enforcement or other authorities where we are legally required to do so.

6. Data retention

We keep your personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including for legal, accounting, and regulatory requirements. Where you do not proceed with a loan, we may keep your application details for a shorter period in line with applicable rules.

7. How we protect your information

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. Access to your data is restricted to staff who need it to provide our services, and they are bound by confidentiality obligations.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you;
  • request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • request deletion of your information in certain circumstances;
  • object to or restrict certain processing; and
  • lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@dreamlandmicro.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

9. Third-party links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites (for example, Google Maps). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites and encourage you to read their privacy policies.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, with the "last updated" date revised accordingly. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

11. Contact us

If you have questions or concerns about this policy or how we handle your information, contact us at info@dreamlandmicro.com or call 0352 293 943.
