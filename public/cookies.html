Cookie Policy

Clear information about the cookies we use and how you can control them.

Last updated: 16 August 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites work properly, remember your preferences, and understand how visitors use the site.

2. Cookies we use

Our website currently uses only a small number of cookies and similar technologies:

  • Strictly necessary cookies: used to keep the website functional and secure, such as remembering your session. These cannot be switched off.
  • Performance cookies: used to understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it. These collect anonymous, aggregated information.

We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies on this website. Some pages may load content from third parties (for example, Google Maps on our contact page or Google Fonts), which may set their own cookies in line with their privacy policies.

3. How to control cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers let you block all cookies, block third-party cookies only, or delete cookies when you close the browser. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect how the website works.

For guidance on managing cookies in your browser, visit your browser's help pages.

4. Contact

If you have questions about this cookie policy, contact us at info@dreamlandmicro.com or call 0352 293 943.
