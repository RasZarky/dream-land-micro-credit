Before you start

What you'll need

  • A valid national ID (Ghana Card, passport, or voter ID)
  • Proof of income or business activity (payslips, bank statements, or sales records)
  • Business registration certificate for the Individual Business Loan
  • Your mobile money or bank account details for disbursal
