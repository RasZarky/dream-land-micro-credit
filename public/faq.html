FAQ

Ghanaian residents aged 18 and above who are employed, self-employed, or run a small business can apply. Both individuals and registered small businesses are welcome. You will need a valid national ID and proof of income or business activity.
Micro loans range from ₵1,000 to ₵10,000 and business loans from ₵5,000 to ₵20,000. Returning customers who repay on time can access larger amounts over time.
We use a simple flat rate on the amount borrowed: 7% for micro loans and 6% for business loans. Emergency loans are priced individually. There are no hidden fees — the rate you agree to is the rate you pay.
No. Our micro and business loans are collateral-free. You just need valid identification and proof of income or business activity.
Most applications are reviewed and approved within 24 hours. Emergency loan applications are fast-tracked and may be approved the same day.
Once your loan is approved, funds are typically disbursed within 24 hours. Disbursal is made to your mobile money account or bank account, depending on what you choose.
A valid national ID (Ghana Card, passport, or voter ID) and proof of income or business activity — for example payslips, bank statements, or sales records. For the Individual Business Loan, proof of business registration is required.
Repayments are made via mobile money or bank transfer, usually in instalments aligned with your income cycle. Your repayment schedule is agreed before you receive the loan.
Yes. You can repay at any time before the due date. Contact our team to confirm your remaining balance and settle early — there are no early repayment penalties.
Please contact us as soon as possible if you anticipate difficulty. We work with borrowers to restructure repayment plans. Late or missed payments may attract charges as set out in your loan agreement and may affect future eligibility.
The Individual Business Loan requires proof of business registration. If you are just starting out, a Micro Loan may be a better fit — you can build a repayment record and access larger business loans later.
Fill in the online application form — it takes about 5 minutes. Our team will contact you to confirm details and request any documents needed.
