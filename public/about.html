About Dreamland

Capital that believes in small businesses

Dreamland Micro Lending exists to close the funding gap for entrepreneurs in Ghana — with fair terms, honest pricing, and support that goes beyond the loan.

Our mission

Why Dreamland exists

Too many promising small businesses in Ghana are held back by a lack of access to fair, timely credit. Dreamland was founded to change that.

Accessibility

Simple applications, collateral-free loans, and fast decisions so capital reaches people who need it most.

Transparency

Flat rates, no hidden fees, and written terms you can understand before you sign anything.

Partnership

We coach borrowers on cashflow and budgeting so that every loan supports lasting growth.

Local roots

Based in Sunyani, we understand local markets, seasonal cashflows, and how businesses really run.

Our story

Built by bankers who know the struggle

Dreamland was founded by a team of finance professionals who spent years in credit management, treasury, and business development. They saw first-hand how often promising businesses were turned away by rigid lending rules — and how much those businesses could achieve with the right support.

So they built a lender that works the way small businesses do: fast, flexible, and honest. Every loan is assessed on real evidence of income and activity, not just paperwork. And every borrower gets support to repay successfully and grow.

Today, Dreamland has disbursed over ₵197,000 in loans to more than 50 borrowers across Ghana, with a focus on sustainable, responsible lending.
