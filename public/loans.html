Eligibility

Who can apply?

  • Ghanaian residents aged 18 years and above
  • Employed, self-employed, or running a small business
  • Valid national ID (Ghana Card, passport, or voter ID)
  • Proof of income or business activity (e.g. payslips, bank statements, or sales records)
  • A registered business is required for the Individual Business Loan

Approval is based on a review of your application and supporting documents. Returning customers who repay on time can access larger amounts and better rates over time.
