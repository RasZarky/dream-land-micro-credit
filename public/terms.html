2. About our services

Dreamland provides micro-loans to individuals and small businesses in Ghana. Our products include Micro Loans, Individual Business Loans, and Emergency Loans. All loans are subject to credit assessment, verification of the information you provide, and a formal loan agreement that we will share with you before disbursal.

3. Eligibility

To apply for a loan you must:

  • be a Ghanaian resident aged 18 years or above;
  • provide valid identification and proof of income or business activity;
  • provide accurate, complete, and truthful information; and
  • meet our credit and affordability criteria.

We reserve the right to decline any application without giving reasons, although we will always explain the outcome to you where required by law.

4. Applications and approval

Submitting an application does not create a binding agreement. Applications are assessed on the information provided and may require supporting documents. Approval is at our sole discretion and is subject to verification. We do not charge any application or processing fees.

5. Loan terms, rates, and fees

Loan amounts, repayment terms, and interest rates depend on the product and your individual assessment. Interest is charged as a simple flat rate on the amount borrowed (currently 7% for Micro Loans and 6% for Individual Business Loans; Emergency Loans are priced individually). There are no hidden fees: all charges will be clearly stated in your loan agreement before you accept it. Charges for late or missed payments, where applicable, are also set out in the loan agreement.

6. Repayment

You agree to repay your loan on the schedule set out in your loan agreement. Repayments are typically made by mobile money or bank transfer. If you anticipate difficulty making a payment, you must contact us promptly so we can discuss restructuring options. Early repayment is permitted without penalty; contact us to confirm your remaining balance.

7. Late or missed payments

Late or missed payments may result in additional charges as set out in your loan agreement, may affect your credit record, and may affect your eligibility for future loans. We will always attempt to contact you before any enforcement action.
